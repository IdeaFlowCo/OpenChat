What's in the box

Every chat-app primitive,
polished and shipped.

💬

Real-time DMs & groups

Socket.IO under the hood. Typing indicators, presence, read receipts in three shades (sent / delivered / read).

🤖

AI assistants in any chat

Invite an AI bot into a DM or group. Permission-gated, scope-aware, and clearly disclosed to all participants.

✨

Pre-send transforms

Tap the sparkle. NVC rewrite, make concise, more formal, more casual, or translate — all before you send.

🎙️

Voice notes

Hold to record, slide to cancel. Plays back inline with a waveform.

📎

Images & link previews

Drop a photo, paste a URL — both render rich in the bubble. Open Graph metadata fetched server-side, cached.

😄

Reactions, replies, forwards

Tap-back any message, quote-reply with a tap, forward across conversations with one shot.

📷

QR add-user & invite

Show your QR to be added instantly. Scan a group invite QR to join. No phone numbers required.

💭

Thoughts stream

A private feed for your facts, decisions, commitments, reminders. Just notes, for now — AI extraction later.

🛡️

Trust & safety primitives

Block, report, account deletion, granular notification controls, Sign in with Apple. App Store-ready.
