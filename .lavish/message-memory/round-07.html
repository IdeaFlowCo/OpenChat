Write together. Keep your own annotations.

Adding in Longevity shares with Dad by default. Choose “Only you” for a private addition. Tags you add to an existing item are private; a lock makes this visible. Try “Preview Dad’s view.”

People and shared streams are different doors.

Roger’s name opens contact details. Your private Roger view brings together captures and notes about him. Longevity is a separate stream you both contribute to. A topic tag describes the item; you don’t need #with-dad.

Start here, wherever the thought came from.

Paste a link or write a note in My notes. Use @ to select recipients or a shared stream. Multiple people default to separate shares; you can explicitly choose a group.

An idea can lead to another idea.

“Add related note” creates a private note with a link back to its starting point. Connections can link people and ideas without publishing your personal observations.

This round explores sharing and invitations; the first OpenChat capture implementation remains private. Public streams and WhatsApp shown here are future concepts. OpenChat retains its existing Ink & Paper design system; the standalone uses the previous neutral/cobalt direction.
