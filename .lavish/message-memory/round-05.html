OpenChat: a place for chat and what survives it

Open Streams directly. Go from All saved to Roger, narrow to #longevity, then jump to the original message. A person can connect several channel-specific conversations without collapsing their histories.

How WhatsApp fits

The combined stream gathers your saved items. “Source conversations” keeps iMessage and WhatsApp distinct, with the channel named on every item. Sending always belongs to an explicitly selected channel.

Reference: Beeper’s combined inbox and network spaces. This concept makes saved knowledge the primary view.

Standalone: open it to find what you saved

All saved, people, search, and an always-visible Add button. Write a note, paste a link, or add a contact detail directly. A small companion runs on your Mac; save inside your normal chats, retrieve here. “Keeps” is a working name, not a branding decision.

What the two concepts share

One capture, original message, person, tags, privacy and pin state. Apple Contacts is a destination for selected structured facts. These are proposed shared records; the mockup does not imply the integration is already live.

One item can be a note, a pin, and a contact detail

Add directly here, reply 🔖 to save from a message, or reply 📌 to save and pin to your private stream, or reply #longevity to file the parent message with a tag. Reactions can use the same mapping when the connector supports them.

Apple’s own content pins are a separate input to investigate. This design does not assume their state is observable.

Contact facts keep their evidence

Click a person’s name on any saved item to open Contact details. Within a person, Stream and Contact details are named views, not a hidden filter. Drew’s address appears in a private contact details with the source date and original wording. “Add to Apple Contacts” is explicit. Existing addresses remain available; a mailing address is not silently labeled “home.”
