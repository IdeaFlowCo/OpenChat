01 / Product shape

A small front door, a reusable core.

My recommendation is an iMessage companion first, exposing OpenChat’s contact, private-profile, and Chat Stream model. A full messaging client is an optional next surface, not a prerequisite for useful capture.

START HERE

Messages companion

Keep Apple Messages. Your Mac observes an explicit capture gesture; phone and desktop show the resulting private memory.

Earns: immediate usefulness without a client migration.
Costs: capture gestures in Messages are visible; retrieval lives elsewhere.

GROW INTO

A minimal messaging app

A familiar inbox, conversation, and optional memory panel. One private Remember action; no compulsory social feed.

Earns: capture and recall in one place.
Costs: bridge reliability, notifications, attachments, sending, and platform parity.

ALSO AVAILABLE IN

OpenChat

The same private memory appears beside existing OpenChat conversations and people. Shared profile content stays separate.

Earns: reuse of identity, clients, and agent access.
Costs: extra product surface unless kept behind a simple Memory door.

“Both” should mean two ways into the same records. Avoid a standalone store that must later be copied into OpenChat.

02 / Capture contract

Save the meaning. Keep the evidence.

Reply, react, or privately remember

In Apple Messages: reply to a specific message with 📌 alone, reply with a hashtag such as #longevity, or use a configured Tapback reaction. These are distinct gestures, all visible to the recipient. The proposed generic emoji reply saves the original to your private stream for that chat. Exact emoji and reaction support must be verified on the target Mac.

In our client: Remember about Maya, privately. Voice intent: “Remember Maya’s message about Portland.” If the target is ambiguous, show candidate messages.

Fast capture, selective review

An explicit gesture saves immediately with Undo. Extracted wording keeps the original quote, sender, date, and channel. Third-person claims, ambiguous subjects, and conflicting facts go to “Needs review.”

“Maya plans to move in November” stays a dated plan. It does not become “Maya lives in Portland.”

Identity, privacy, failure, and undo rules
  • Only the owner’s configured reaction or reply triggers capture. Other people’s reactions, quoted hashtags, and historic backlog do not auto-import.
  • An actual reply must resolve to a source message ID. If that relationship is missing, request selection rather than guessing the preceding message.
  • A reaction means capture, not an instruction to execute message text. No message content may change sharing permissions or trigger outbound actions.
  • Attach memory to the person the content is about, not automatically the sender. “My sister lives in Portland” requires subject review.
  • Resolve phone/email/channel handles to a stable person ID. Suggest uncertain matches; never merge on display name alone. Keep reversible identity links and separate group threads.
  • Deduplicate reconnects using owner + source account + message ID + capture intent. Different trigger methods on the same source update one capture.
  • Removing the capture reaction withdraws a still-unedited gesture capture. For an edited note, flag the removed marker and offer Forget. Undo/Forget removes this memory, not the original message.
  • Message edits mark the source as changed and offer refresh; deletion marks it unavailable. No silent rewriting of reviewed memory. Forget propagates to derived indexes and linked views.
  • If the Mac is offline, show cached history and the last sync time. Save local capture intents as pending. Never show a send as delivered or blindly resend an uncertain attempt.
  • Start watching from activation. Historical capture requires an explicit import or selection. Bound any context window used for extraction.
  • Profiles are private to the capturing user. Sharing is a separate previewed action; a public profile never reads private memories by default.
03 / Multiple networks

Unify the person. Preserve the conversation.

Default: separate threads

One person entry, an owner-private contact card and person notes across channels, and explicit iMessage / WhatsApp tabs. Preserve each thread’s participants, unread state, replies, and timestamps.

Optional: all history

An interleaved history with a channel label on every message. The composer always names its destination. Reading a message on another network never silently changes where your draft will send.

Group conversations and their streams remain separate, even if their membership overlaps. A cross-channel person view can aggregate authorized entries, but each item keeps its original chat scope and audience. Reading the combined view does not automatically mark every underlying thread as read.

Contact and stream vision

A full personal address book inside OpenChat.

Apple Contacts-equivalent completeness is part of the target: people need not have OpenChat accounts. Their official profile and your editable contact card remain distinct layers.

Contact information

Names, nicknames and pronunciation; multiple labeled phone numbers, emails and postal addresses; organization, role, websites, social handles, birthday, dates, relationships, photo and notes. Custom labels, search, reversible linking, merge review and vCard import/export belong in the vision.

Knowledge about a person

Preferences, interests, goals, projects, introductions, family context and things to follow up on. Keep the original words and date. “Interested in longevity” is an interest; an article they sent is not evidence of a medical condition or endorsement.

Knowledge from a chat

Articles, videos, papers, recommendations, ideas, decisions, questions and commitments. Your chat with Dad becomes a browsable longevity collection, with tags, original links, who sent each item, and private annotations.

Which information belongs where, and what could be shared?
  • Address / phone / email: your contact card, private by default. Share selected contact fields deliberately with a named recipient; never silently publish them.
  • Dietary or communication preference: private person note; optionally a structured field when useful. The person can choose to include their own preference in their shared profile.
  • Professional role / website / public project: contact or profile detail; a reasonable candidate for owner-controlled shared profile information. Capture alone does not authorize publication.
  • Research link / book / restaurant: chat stream and topic tags. Share the resource with the chat if desired, while keeping personal annotations private.
  • “I’ll send you the paper Friday”: a chat-scoped commitment with person, source and due date. Reminders or notifications are a separate opt-in behavior.
  • Health, family, finances or relationship context: preserve privately when explicitly saved. Do not turn it into a public-profile suggestion by default or infer diagnoses from topics discussed.
  • Passwords, recovery codes and payment credentials: direct to a dedicated vault rather than treating them as contact-card or stream fields.

Two audiences that must not be conflated: “Only you” versus “Shared with Dad” for a chat entry; “My private contact card for Maya” versus “Maya’s owner-controlled shared profile” for person data.

A shared entry uses a reviewed copy or explicit shared revision. Later private annotations do not become visible because the source resource was previously shared.

Literal hashtag and emoji reply rules

Replying 📌 alone saves the replied-to message in this chat’s private stream, with no topic tag required. Use a configured capture emoji rather than treating every emoji reply as a command. A standalone emoji without a reply target does not guess the previous message. This is proposed behavior, not a shipped bridge feature.

Replying #longevity to Dad’s message saves the parent message, its link, and your tag to that chat’s stream. It does not save a useless note consisting only of “#longevity.” Any topic tag can work; there is no required taxonomy.

Optional reserved actions #contact / #address target structured contact fields; #remember targets person knowledge. Their exact vocabulary is proposed, not yet an approved parser change. Topic tags such as #longevity organize the result. Ordinary inline hashtags retain existing OpenChat behavior.

For an address already present, a new explicit capture can add an empty field immediately with Undo. Conflicts, multiple subjects, or an unclear home/work label require a compact review. Sync back to Apple Contacts is a separate selected operation with a stable record link and conflict handling.

Existing OpenChat functionality verified: literal reply-tag capture of the parent message; free-form topic tags; Chat Stream / Context with per-entry audiences; private profile capture; saved people without accounts. The current public vCard projection is narrow, so it does not establish full Apple Contacts parity. The iMessage bridge, structured contact completeness and optional device-contact sync are the proposed extensions.

Compatibility: existing OpenChat reply-tag captures can be shared with the chat. External iMessage capture will explicitly default to Only you, without silently changing the current OpenChat audience contract.

04 / Relationship to Vision

The profile is one view of a captured note.

Vision’s “note that” idea and this message gesture express the same intent: keep a moment, preserve its context, find it later. That is a useful shared primitive without making Vision into a messaging app.

Capture once

A source message, a private owner, a person link, a dated note, and optional extracted fields.

Recall in context

OpenChat shows it beside Maya. A minimal client shows it in Memory. Vision shows it in your stream and search.

One editable record

Use a shared stable note ID and ownership contract. Editing or forgetting changes the same record everywhere.

Existing direction, unfinished plumbing: OpenChat’s Thoughts design names NoteStream as the long-term canonical note store; OpenChat still uses its own Thought records. Vision has a real desktop capture path, but its iMessage outbox adapter is a stub. This proposal does not assume cross-app sync already works.

05 / Feasibility and first slice

Familiar like Messages. Parity must be earned.

iMessage

A Mac bridge is a plausible path: BlueBubbles documents database observation and AppleScript sending. Its richer interactions use optional private APIs. Prove incoming reaction identity, reply linkage, edit/removal events, and reconnection on the actual macOS version first.

Apple’s TelephonyMessagingKit covers SMS/MMS/RCS, with eligibility restrictions; it is not an iMessage inbox API. The proposed phone client depends on the Mac bridge.

WhatsApp

Design the channel adapter boundary now; select and validate the personal-account connector separately. Do not assume a business messaging API exposes a personal inbox.

Identity mapping, incoming reactions, history, notification behavior, and reconnect limits need connector-specific validation before enabling it.

The first implementation milestone and acceptance checks
  1. Read-only spike against an explicitly selected test thread: reliably identify your capture reaction, its source, changes, and reconnect replay.
  2. Implement one owner-private memory record with original excerpt, source time, person assignment, deduplication, review, and Undo.
  3. Make it reachable on phone and desktop through a small memory screen. Keep Apple Messages as the chat client for this milestone.
  4. Prove the loop: capture once, reconnect without duplication, find the note under the correct person, view its source, and forget it across views.
  5. Only then add client sending, delivery states, attachments, unread/notification semantics, and group parity. Validate each capability on Mac and iPhone; do not label the result “identical” prematurely.

Before implementation: decide the authoritative store/API using the existing NoteStream direction and OpenChat’s current schema. Share identifiers and deletion semantics from the first migration; avoid uncontrolled dual writes.

06 / Direction for the next pass

Where should we push the design?

Confirmed direction: private-first contact information, private person knowledge, and per-chat streams. Selected material may later be shared. Product packaging stays reversible. These controls queue feedback; they do not authorize deployment.
